# Day 04 – Q3: Healthcare Scheduling

## Question

Draft an objective, 3 key results and WSJF calculation for the given industry scenario.

### Scenario

**Healthcare Scheduling:** Patients wait an average of 12 minutes on hold to book an appointment.

The objective should describe the desired outcome, not the fix.

---

## Objective

Improve the healthcare appointment-booking experience by making scheduling faster, more accessible, and more reliable for patients.

---

## Key Results

### KR1 – Reduce Patient Waiting Time

Reduce average phone waiting time from **12 minutes to 3 minutes**.

### KR2 – Improve Appointment-Booking Success

Increase the percentage of patients who successfully book an appointment on their first attempt from **75% to 95%**.

### KR3 – Improve Patient Satisfaction

Increase appointment-booking satisfaction from **70% to 90%**.

---

## WSJF Calculation

### Formula

WSJF = Cost of Delay / Job Size

Cost of Delay = Business Value + Time Criticality + Risk Reduction / Opportunity Enablement

A relative scoring scale of 1–10 is used.

| Initiative | Business Value | Time Criticality | Risk Reduction / Opportunity | Cost of Delay | Job Size | WSJF |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Reduce patient waiting time | 9 | 10 | 7 | 26 | 5 | 5.20 |
| Improve booking success rate | 9 | 8 | 8 | 25 | 6 | 4.17 |
| Improve patient satisfaction | 7 | 6 | 6 | 19 | 4 | 4.75 |

### Calculations

**Reduce patient waiting time**

26 / 5 = **5.20**

**Improve booking success rate**

25 / 6 = **4.17**

**Improve patient satisfaction**

19 / 4 = **4.75**

### Priority

1. Reduce patient waiting time – **5.20**
2. Improve patient satisfaction – **4.75**
3. Improve booking success rate – **4.17**
