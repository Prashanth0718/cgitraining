# Day 10 - Question 1

## Ongoing Monitoring vs Effective Challenge

### Real-life scenario: Customer Support GenAI

Let's take a customer support GenAI tool. The tool reads a customer's complaint and suggests a reply for the support agent.

### Ongoing Monitoring

After the tool is deployed, the team keeps checking how it is performing. They may look at things like incorrect replies, customer complaints, how often agents change the AI's answers, and whether the error rate is increasing.

For example, if the tool normally gives incorrect answers in 2% of cases but suddenly starts giving them in 10%, the team should notice this and investigate.

So, **monitoring is mainly about watching the system over time and seeing whether anything has changed or gone wrong.**

### Effective Challenge

Effective challenge is a little different. Here, someone actually questions the system and its decisions instead of just recording what happened.

For example, suppose an independent review team notices that the AI is giving poor responses to customers with billing complaints. They would look into why this is happening and question whether the system should continue working that way.

They could ask the team to change the system, add another control, or stop the use case if the problem is serious.

So, **effective challenge is about asking whether the system's behaviour is actually acceptable and whether the team has a good reason for using it that way.**
